## Role-Based Access

Components are classified by access tier:

- **Master components**: visible only when `user.role === 'master'`.
- **Sister components**: available to sister users and masters.
- **User components**: accessible to all authenticated users.
- **Guest components**: complete features for unauthenticated/readonly access.

Examples:

- `SystemHealthDashboard.tsx` – Master-only
- `QVillage.tsx` – User/Sister
- `SharePanel.tsx` – Sister/User
