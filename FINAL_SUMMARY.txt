🆘 QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

"No connection" to Ollama:
  curl http://localhost:11434/api/tags
  ollama serve > /dev/null 2>&1 &

"Model not found":
  ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:3b

"Port already in use":
  pkill ollama
  ollama serve > /dev/null 2>&1 &

"Continue can't connect":
  1. Verify Ollama is running (see above)
  2. Check config.json has correct settings
  3. Reload VS Code (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R)
  4. Restart Continue panel

Full troubleshooting guide in SETUP_CHECKLIST.md
